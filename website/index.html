Everything you need to clip, nothing you don't

Download only the clip

Pick the start and end on a timeline, or type exact times. Only that range is saved, so long videos take seconds, not hours.

Preview and zoom

Look at the picture at any moment while you set the cut, and zoom the timeline in to land on the exact second.

Crop for Reels and Shorts

Turn a wide video into 9:16, square or 4:5 with a draggable crop box. The crop is built into the file you download.

Fast, in parts

Long clips are split into parts that download at the same time, then joined. In our test, a three-minute clip took about 15 seconds instead of 95.

Queue, pause and resume

Line up several clips, run a few at once, and pause any of them. Finished parts are kept, so resuming never starts over.

Video or audio

Save MP4 at the quality you choose, or just the sound as MP3, M4A or Opus. Works with YouTube and over a thousand other sites.

Questions

Is Trimly free?

Yes. It's open source and free to use. The code is on GitHub.

Does it download the whole video first?

No. Trimly asks the server for only the time range you chose, so a thirty-second clip from a two-hour video downloads about thirty seconds of data.

Which sites work?

YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, X and more than a thousand others, because Trimly is built on yt-dlp. Private or age-restricted videos can work if you let Trimly use your browser's sign-in in Settings.

Why do cropped clips take a little longer?

Cropping re-encodes the video. Trimly does it in parts at the same time, so it stays quick.

Is it okay to download any video?

Only download and share content you have the right to use. Trimly is a tool, and what you do with it is up to you and the rules of the sites involved.
